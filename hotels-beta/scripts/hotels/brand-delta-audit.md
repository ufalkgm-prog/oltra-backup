## Notes on methodology

- Properties were matched on hotel name similarity + city/country (fuzzy matching).
- Properties explicitly listed as "coming soon", "opening soon", or in the development pipeline are **excluded** unless they opened before July 2026.
- Belmond trains and river cruises are excluded (Orient-Express, Hiram Bingham, etc.).
- For The Luxury Collection (130+ properties), only the most editorially significant missing properties are listed.
- Sources: brand official websites (aman.com, belmond.com, rosewoodhotels.com), Wikipedia brand pages, starsdesk.com, liveluxetravelco.com, and web searches.
